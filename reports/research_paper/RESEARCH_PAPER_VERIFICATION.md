## 6. Unresolved limitations

1. **PDF pages not viewed directly.** No PDF renderer is available in this environment. The layout was checked by
   rendering the identical HTML to screenshots before printing, and the PDF's structure (header, trailer, 23 pages)
   was checked. Open the PDF once to confirm page breaks.
2. **Reconstructed lead time.** The lead time is an inference, strongly supported but not confirmed by the data owner.
3. **Assumption-driven estimates.** All financial and delivery-time impacts depend on unverified assumptions (Table 6).
4. **Approximate prediction ranges.** The ranges are nominally 90% but have measured coverage of 100%; they are wide.
5. **Superseded files kept.** Version-1 files are preserved in `archive_superseded_v1_2026-10-22/` for the record. They
   must not be cited.
